How it works

01

Add your assets

Drag clips, SFX, PNGs, transitions, or overlays into cards.

02

Open instantly

Press the backtick key (`) to open Clip Deck anytime.

03

Send to timeline

Click a card or use a hotkey to instantly bring assets into your edit.

Packed With Powerful Tools

Everything you need to organize, access, and create—so you can edit faster and stay in your creative flow.

01

One-click
clip launching

Fire any video, audio, or image clip straight into your Premiere timeline from a visual deck of cards. No digging through bins.
02

Add Any
Asset type

Add any type of asset you need in Clip Deck:
  • Video Clips
  • Audio Clips
  • Images
  • Transitions
  • Effects
  • Timeline Clips
  • MOGRTs
  • Text
03

Smart Insert

Clip Deck detects empty layers and inserts clips there to avoid overwriting and collision of clips.
04

Libraries & Decks

Organize everything into libraries, each with multiple decks (pages) of cards. Build a deck per project, client, or style.
05

Full
Customization

Rearrange cards within a deck or across decks, and color-code everything — your layout, your way.
06

Custom
thumbnails

Image/video thumbnails, a custom thumbnail picker, and per-card color coding for instant visual recognition.
07

Hotkey
Navigation

Navigate through Clip Deck's Libraries and Decks with custom hotkeys.
08

Clip Deck Search

Search across every library, deck, and tool at once — and fire results straight from the keyboard.
09
FX

User Effects

Save a clip's full effect stack as a reusable recipe card and apply it to any other clip with one click.
10

User Transitions

Capture and re-fire your favorite transitions across projects.
11

Save Timeline
Clips

Turn clips and assets from your timeline into reusable cards you can fire again anytime.
12

Pixabay Browser

Browse and import royalty-free SFX and stock footage directly to your timeline, downloaded straight into your project folder.
13

Youtube Browser

Pull up reference videos inside the panel without leaving Premiere.
14

Most Used &
Recently Used

Global views that surface your go-to assets across every library, with usage that survives moves and swaps.
15

Import & Export
Libraries

Share or back up entire libraries via portable .cdk files, cross-platform.
